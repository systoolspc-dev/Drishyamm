## 2. Architecture and training recipe

| item | value |
|---|---|
| architecture | YOLO26-M (end2end NMS-free, reg_max 1), 21.78 M params, 75.0 GFLOPs at 640 (unfused) |
| base | `yolo26m.pt` COCO-pretrained (passed via `--model yolo26m.pt`; the script default is `yolo26s.pt`) |
| imgsz / batch / epochs | 640 / 64 / **100, completed** (patience 40, best epoch 85) |
| optimizer | **AdamW, lr0 0.001667** (`0.002*5/(4+nc)`), lrf 0.01, **cosine LR**, wd 5e-4, warmup 3 |
| why AdamW | per `train_yolo26.py` and `train.log`: `optimizer=auto` picks MuSGD on long runs, and MuSGD crashes in Ultralytics 8.4.137 (`muon.py:99`, `.view()` on a non-contiguous tensor). `train.log` (15:53) holds that crash trace. Runs `accident_yolo26m` (15:49) and `-2` (15:52) have no results and no weights |
| augmentation | mosaic 1.0 (close 10), hsv 0.015/0.7/0.4, translate 0.1, scale 0.5, fliplr 0.5, no mixup or copy-paste. `save_period 10` (epoch snapshots) |
| wall time | 29,704 s (about 8.3 h) |
| Ultralytics | 8.4.137, checkpoint 2026-09-01 22:59 IST |

  to 0.35 (minimum 0.342 at epoch 88).
- The gap between train cls loss and val cls loss widens late in training: mild overfit after about 85.

**Normalized confusion matrix (valid):**
- Accident: 0.97 correct, 0.03 missed.
- Non Accident: 0.92 correct, 0.07 missed, 0.01 called Accident.
- Background false positives: **81 % come out as "Accident"** and 19 % as Non Accident. When the model is
  wrong on clutter, it is wrong in the alarming direction.

## 4. Inference

```bash
yolo detect predict model=project/runs/detect/accident_yolo26m-3/weights/best.pt source=cam.mp4 imgsz=640 conf=0.25
```
- **Preprocessing:** letterbox 640, RGB, divide by 255.
- **Postprocessing:** end-to-end output `(1, 300, 6)` `[x1, y1, x2, y2, score, cls]`. No NMS; score filter
  only. Alert on cls 0 only.
- The repo's own exporter path: `train_yolo26.py --export engine` (it calls `export(format=…, half=True)`).
- **Platform status: NOT integrated.** There are no accident references in `smart-cam-ai/` or
  `smart-cam-backend/src`.

## 5. Known weaknesses

- **Probable frame-level leakage, unverified (filename evidence only).**
  - 15 clip prefixes (`c1`, `c10` … `c13`) appear in train, valid and test.
  - 938 of 1,401 unique valid stems and 592 of 830 test stems also occur in train, though none
    byte-identical.
  - The 70/20/10 split looks random per image, so adjacent frames of one crash clip sit on both sides.
  - The 0.96 mAP50 is likely optimistic for new cameras.
- **Class imbalance** of about 6.6:1 (Accident vs Non Accident). Real CCTV has the opposite imbalance:
  almost every frame is "no accident". The dataset has essentially **no pure-background frames**.
- "Accident" is a scene-level, temporal event (impact, debris, vehicles stopped at angles). A single-frame
  detector learns appearance cues such as crumpled bodywork and overturned vehicles. Parked damaged cars and
  scrapyards will fire. The crash moment itself is often missed.
- Night and IR coverage is unknown.

## 6. Improvement ideas (embedded)

1. **Honest re-evaluation.** Re-split by clip prefix. Score on 24 h of normal traffic from your own cameras
   and count false alerts per camera-day. That is the operational metric.
2. **Hard negatives.** Add thousands of empty-label frames: busy junctions, traffic jams, parked or
   breakdown vehicles, roadworks, night glare.
3. **Temporal stage.**
   - Use this detector as a trigger.
   - Confirm with track kinematics: sudden deceleration to zero, overlapping vehicle tracks, persons
     appearing on the carriageway.
   - Or confirm with a short-clip classifier (e.g. the VideoMAE approach in `../fight-detection-rtdetr-videomae`).
   - Require K-of-N detections on a stable region for 2 to 3 s, then one alert per incident. See
     `../../docs/DETECTION_DECISION_ALERT_FRAMEWORK.md`.
4. **Distil** to `yolo26s` or `yolo26n` (`distill_model=<this best.pt>`) for edge use. Accident cues are
   large, so 640 or even 512 is fine.
5. **Export:**
   - `yolo export model=best.pt format=onnx imgsz=640 simplify=True`
   - `format=engine half=True`
   - `format=engine int8=True data=accident_data.yaml`
   - On Ultralytics 8.4.137 `quantize=8` is the new spelling.
